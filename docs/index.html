Have one of these on a shelf? Can you lend it for a semester?

DRACO Lab has a time sensitive request and needs to stand up a calibrated power/performance measurement bench for a benchmarking engagement — and we would rather borrow from colleagues and provide them back, at our expense, calibrated equipment.

The deal

We need the instruments below for approximately ten weeks (Oct 2026 – Jan 2027). Every borrowed instrument comes back with a ISO/IEC 17025, NIST-traceable calibration performed at project expense. Loans are logged under chain-of-custody procedures, instruments live in secure DRACO Lab space (HEC), and lenders are acknowledged as requested.

How it works

  1. 1 Offer Two minutes on the form below, even for a near-equivalent.
  2. 2 Handoff We log serials and condition, agree on the return date, and handle transport. UCF property procedures apply throughout.
  3. 3 Return On schedule, with a fresh calibration certificate. The engagement is a single measurement session with a hard end date; instruments come back to you in a timely way.

What we need

You'll find the equipment we're looking for here. Please note that we can not say what the instrument will be doing on the bench: the engagement is under an NDA and the measurement design sits inside it. Your equipment will not be modified beyond standardized calibration. If a detail would change your decision, ask — we can say more in a room than on a public page.

What you get back

A loan should not be a favor you regret. The calibration is the headline of the return side, but it is not the whole of it. Items marked "ask on the form" happen only if you ask for them — they are offers, not obligations.

Select any of the "ask on the form" cards you would like. Ticking one is not a commitment, and the rest happen whether you ask or not.

  • Standard

    A fresh accredited calibration

    Details

    Every instrument goes back with an ISO/IEC 17025, NIST-traceable calibration performed at project expense. The certificate travels with it, and so do the as-found and as-left data — the as-found record tells you whether the measurements you already took were inside tolerance.

  • Standard

    A condition and custody record

    Details

    Serial numbers, photographs at handoff and at return, and a chain-of-custody log. It is the paperwork your department property manager asks for, prepared without you having to prepare it.

  • Standard

    A service check while it is here

    Details

    Firmware brought current, probe and lead sets verified against the instrument, and a written note of anything worn or missing from the accessory kit. Small work, but it is work you would otherwise have to schedule.

  • Standard

    Transport, custody, and a fixed return date

    Details

    We collect the instrument and we bring it back. The loan runs under UCF property procedures from pickup to return, and the return date is agreed in writing before anything moves.

  • Standard

    First call when the direction reverses

    Details

    Lenders go to the front of the queue for DRACO Lab instruments, bench space, and measurement help. We keep that ledger and we honor it.

  • Opt-in

    Acknowledgement, if you want it

    Details

    Named in the acknowledgements of resulting publications, listed on this page, both, or neither. The default is neither. You pick the wording, and you can withdraw it at any point before a manuscript goes out.

  • Details

    Once the measurement chain is characterized we can put your board, module, or device on it and take a real measurement with you. The bench is expensive to assemble and it is being assembled anyway.

  • Details

    The SCPI automation, the capture harness, and the written measurement protocol are released openly. Lenders get them first, with a walkthrough, rather than waiting for the repository to go public.

  • Details

    A student from your group is welcome in the setup and calibration sessions. Precision power measurement transfers badly from a manual, and there are few places on campus to learn it with instruments in front of you.

  • Details

    We will write a facilities, collaboration, or shared-instrumentation letter for your next submission, and we will write it properly rather than from a template. This is a standing offer, not a one-time trade.
